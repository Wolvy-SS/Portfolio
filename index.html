About

Unity Developer with 6+ years of experience building interactive 2D, 3D, AR/VR, and AI-powered applications.

Skilled in C# programming, cross-platform development, and rapid prototyping.

Known for pushing the limits of interaction design, optimization, and user experience across platforms (PC, Android, iOS, Web).

I'm always exploring new tech and building real-time experiences that are intuitive, scalable, and immersive.
