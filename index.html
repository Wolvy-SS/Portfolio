About

I'm a Unity Developer with 6+ years of experience building high-performance 2D, 3D, AR/VR, and AI-powered interactive applications.

Over the years, I've developed indoor navigation systems, 3D visualization platforms, interactive educational applications, science experiment simulations, and large-scale 3D wayfinding systems for expos and booth experiences. I've also integrated text-to-speech and speech-to-text solutions to create more accessible and intelligent user interactions.

My expertise lies in C# programming, scalable Unity architecture, cross-platform deployment (PC, Android, iOS, WebGL), and immersive interaction design. I focus heavily on performance optimization, intuitive user experience, and building systems that are both technically robust and user-friendly.

I enjoy exploring emerging technologies and experimenting with new tools to transform ideas into real-time interactive solutions. Innovation, problem-solving, and thinking beyond conventional approaches drive my work. I thrive on challenges that require creativity, analytical thinking, and building something meaningful from the ground up.

My goal is to continue pushing the boundaries of immersive technology while creating scalable, impactful, and forward-thinking digital experiences.
